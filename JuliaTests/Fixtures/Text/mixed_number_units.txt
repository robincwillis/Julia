Everything Pancakes

Serves 4

1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
2 1/4 tsp baking powder
½ tsp fine salt
1 1/4 cups whole milk
2 tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter, melted
1 large egg, lightly beaten
2-3 tbsp sugar, to taste
⅓ cup blueberries, optional
1 tbsp neutral oil, for the pan

Whisk the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar in a large bowl.
Beat the milk, melted butter and egg together, then stir into the dry mix.
Rest the batter for 10 minutes while the pan heats.
Cook 1/4 cup of batter per pancake, 2 minutes a side.
