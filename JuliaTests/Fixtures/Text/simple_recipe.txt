Lemon Garlic Roast Chicken

Serves 4
Prep time: 20 minutes
Cook time: 1 hour 15 minutes

Ingredients

1 whole chicken, about 4 lbs
3 tablespoons olive oil
4 cloves garlic, minced
2 lemons, one juiced and one quartered
1 tablespoon kosher salt
2 teaspoons black pepper
1/2 cup chicken stock
3 sprigs fresh thyme

Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425F and set a rack in the lower third.
2. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and season the cavity generously with salt and pepper.
3. Whisk together the olive oil, garlic, and lemon juice, then rub it all over the skin.
4. Stuff the cavity with the quartered lemon and thyme sprigs.
5. Roast for 45 minutes, then pour the stock into the pan and continue roasting for another 30 minutes.
6. Rest the chicken for 15 minutes before carving.

Notes

Save the pan drippings for gravy.
