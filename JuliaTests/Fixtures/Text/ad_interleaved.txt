Weeknight Sesame Noodles

Serves 2
Total time: 15 minutes

200 g dried egg noodles
3 tbsp tahini
2 tbsp soy sauce

1 tbsp rice vinegar
1 tsp honey
1 clove garlic, grated
2 spring onions, sliced
1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Continue reading below

Cook the noodles according to the packet, then drain and rinse under cold water.
Whisk the tahini, soy, vinegar, honey and garlic with 2 tbsp warm water.

Toss the noodles through the sauce until every strand is coated.
Top with spring onions and sesame seeds.
