Three-Layer Celebration Chocolate Cake

A tall, fudgy cake for birthdays. The crumb stays moist for days.

Serves 12
Prep: 45 minutes
Bake: 35 minutes
Total: 3 hours including cooling

For the cake layers:

340 g plain flour
400 g caster sugar
90 g cocoa powder
2 tsp baking soda
1 tsp baking powder
1 tsp fine salt
3 large eggs, room temperature
360 ml buttermilk
120 ml vegetable oil
2 tsp vanilla extract
240 ml hot strong coffee

For the buttercream:

250 g unsalted butter, softened
500 g icing sugar
75 g cocoa powder
60 ml double cream
1 tsp vanilla extract
Pinch of salt

For the ganache drip:

150 g dark chocolate, chopped
150 ml double cream

Heat the oven to 175C and grease three 20 cm round tins.
Whisk the flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder and salt together.
In a second bowl beat the eggs, buttermilk, oil and vanilla until smooth.
Pour the wet mixture into the dry and stir until only just combined.
Add the hot coffee and stir again — the batter will be very loose.
Divide between the three tins and bake 30 to 35 minutes.
Cool in the tins 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack and cool completely.
Beat the butter for the buttercream until pale, about 5 minutes.
Add the icing sugar and cocoa in three additions, beating between each.
Pour in the cream, vanilla and salt, then beat until light.
Level the cooled layers and stack them with buttercream between each.
Coat the outside thinly, chill 20 minutes, then apply a second coat.
Warm the cream for the ganache and pour it over the chopped chocolate.
Stir until glossy, cool to room temperature, then drip around the top edge.

Tip: the coffee deepens the chocolate flavour and is not detectable.
Make ahead: the layers freeze well, wrapped, for up to a month.
Keeps 4 days in an airtight tin at room temperature.

Adapted from a recipe by Claire Ptak, Violet Bakery.
