SPICED CARROT & LENTIL SOUP

Serves 4     Prep 10 mins     Cook 25 mins

2 tsp cumin seeds
pinch chilli flakes
2 tbsp olive
oil
600 g carrots, washed and coarsely grated
140 g split red lentils
1 l hot vegetable stock
125 ml milk
plain yogurt and naan bread, to serve

1 Heat a large saucepan and dry-fry 2 tsp cumin seeds and a
pinch of chilli flakes for 1 min, or until they start to jump
around the pan and release their aromas.

2 Scoop out about half with a spoon and set aside. Add the oil,
carrot, lentils, stock and milk to the pan and bring to the boil.

3 Simmer for 15 mins until the lentils have swollen and softened.

4 Whizz the soup with a stick blender or in a food processor
until smooth. Season to taste and finish with a swirl of yogurt
and a scattering of the reserved toasted spices.

Recipe from Good Food magazine, March 2009
