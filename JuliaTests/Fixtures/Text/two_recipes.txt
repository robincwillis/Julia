Simple Vinaigrette

Makes about 120 ml

3 tbsp red wine vinegar
1 tsp Dijon mustard
1 small shallot, minced
120 ml olive oil
Salt and black pepper

Whisk the vinegar, mustard and shallot together.
Add the oil in a slow stream, whisking, until emulsified.
Season to taste.

Little Gem Salad with Vinaigrette

Serves 4

3 heads little gem lettuce, halved lengthways
50 g Parmesan, shaved
2 tbsp toasted hazelnuts, crushed
Vinaigrette, from above

Arrange the lettuce halves cut side up on a platter.
Spoon over the vinaigrette.
Finish with the Parmesan and hazelnuts.
