The Only Banana Bread Recipe You Will Ever Need

Okay friends, I need to tell you about my grandmother. Every Sunday of my
childhood she would pull a loaf out of the oven and the whole house smelled
like brown sugar and rain. I have been chasing that smell for fifteen years.
I have tried adding sour cream. I have tried browning the butter. I have
tried, once, memorably, adding a whole cup of espresso, which we will not be
discussing today.

Anyway. Last winter I finally cracked it, and the secret is embarrassingly
simple: you have to let the bananas go further than feels reasonable. Black.
Genuinely black. If your bananas look presentable, they are not ready.

Jump to Recipe

Ingredients

3 very ripe bananas, mashed
115 g melted butter
150 g sugar
1 egg, beaten
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp baking soda
Pinch of salt
190 g plain flour

Instructions

Preheat the oven to 175C and butter a 9x5 inch loaf tin.
Mix the mashed bananas and melted butter in a large bowl.
Stir in the sugar, egg and vanilla, then the baking soda and salt.
Fold in the flour last, mixing only until no dry streaks remain.
Pour into the tin and bake for 60 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
Cool on a rack for 15 minutes before turning out.
